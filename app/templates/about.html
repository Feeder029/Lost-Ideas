Lost Ideas

"Every idea starts somewhere. But some just get left behind."

Why does this exist?

We all have ideas that never become anything. A project we started and abandoned. A thought we wrote down and forgot. Something we wanted to build, but never did.

Lost Ideas is an archive for those things.

Instead of letting an unfinished idea disappear, you can leave it here. Someone else might find it, see something in it, and take it somewhere you couldn't.
